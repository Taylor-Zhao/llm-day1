## Scoring Rubric / 评分标准

- Accuracy 准确性（1-5）
1 = mostly wrong, 3 = partially correct, 5 = highly correct and reliable
1 = 大部分不正确，3 = 部分正确，5 = 基本准确可靠

- Structure Quality 结构化程度（1-5）
1 = chaotic, 3 = acceptable structure, 5 = clear hierarchy and easy to scan
1 = 混乱，3 = 结构一般，5 = 层次清晰、易读

- Actionability 可执行性（1-5）
1 = hard to execute, 3 = some actionable steps, 5 = clear and directly executable
1 = 难以落地，3 = 有部分可执行步骤，5 = 可直接执行
